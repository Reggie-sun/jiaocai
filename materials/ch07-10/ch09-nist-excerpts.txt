L615@P25: The GOVERN function:
L616@P25: • cultivates and implements a culture of risk management within organizations designing, developing, deploying, evaluating, or acquiring AI systems;
L623@P25: values and principles, and enables organizational practices and competencies for the
L624@P25: individuals involved in acquiring, training, deploying, and monitoring such systems;
L625@P25: and
L626@P25: • addresses full product lifecycle and associated processes, including legal and other
L627@P25: issues concerning use of third-party software or hardware systems and data.

L629@P26: GOVERN is a cross-cutting function that is infused throughout AI risk management and
L630@P26: enables the other functions of the process. Aspects of GOVERN, especially those related to
L631@P26: compliance or evaluation, should be integrated into each of the other functions. Attention
L632@P26: to governance is a continual and intrinsic requirement for effective AI risk management
L633@P26: over an AI system’s lifespan and the organization’s hierarchy.
L634@P26: Strong governance can drive and enhance internal practices and norms to facilitate organizational risk culture. Governing authorities can determine the overarching policies that
L635@P26: direct an organization’s mission, goals, values, culture, and risk tolerance. Senior leadership sets the tone for risk management within an organization, and with it, organizational
L636@P26: culture. Management aligns the technical aspects of AI risk management to policies and
L637@P26: operations. Documentation can enhance transparency, improve human review processes,
L638@P26: and bolster accountability in AI system teams.
