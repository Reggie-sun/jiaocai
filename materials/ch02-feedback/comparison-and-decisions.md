The original outline's statements that AI “replaces” manual decomposition or automatically discovers nonlinear truth are treated as candidate framing, not universal technical facts. The manuscript instead says that learned methods can form richer candidate representations or adapt in a defined task, while results depend on data, objective, constraints, evaluation, and deployment scope. School-specific claims and commitments are not used; campus references remain generic teaching simulations.
