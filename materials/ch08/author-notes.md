## Authored teaching material and interpretation

- All personal cases in Sections 8.1–8.6 are labeled or framed as fictional. RiverCSV and MapleDB behaviors/evidence cards are invented solely for practice; they do not refer to or describe actual products.
- All numerical inputs in E01, E06 and E07 are synthetic. The E06 fixture and E07 baseline/transfer CSV are independently recalculated by `verify_exercises.py`; candidate values are called items for review, not invalid records.
- E05 supplies a standalone HTML page, an empty four-field record and an observation log. The fixture source and field order are machine-checked; no actual browser run or learner observation is claimed. Paper-only work is explicitly marked “未观察.”
- The 14-day local Git challenge, 445-minute plan, its 35-minute buffer and weekly totals are author-designed teaching material, not research results or a record of execution. The printed learner log is labeled “虚构的填写示例” and “不是实际执行记录”.
- The recommendations about task analysis, source evaluation, choosing tools, maintaining a PLN, and adapting an IDP are the author’s instructional synthesis. They are phrased as methods for checking and adjusting learning, not empirical promises of employment, retention or performance outcomes.
- No independent empirical claim is made for a named “Feynman technique.” Self-explanation is discussed only with the evidence limitations summarized in [2].
