# Chapter 7 Figure Plan

These six entries correspond to the existing `<!-- FIGURE:7-N -->` markers in `manuscripts/ch07/ch07.md`. The figure labels and reader-facing guidance already follow each marker in the manuscript. This file records the intended meaning and evidence boundaries; it does not supply image assets.

## Figure 7-1 — 能力信念怎样影响学习行动

- **Insertion:** 7.1.2, after the paragraph that introduces event, interpretation, goal, action, and new evidence.
- **Form:** A five-stage feedback flow: event → interpretation → goal → action → new evidence → revised interpretation. Show task demands, time, and support as contextual influences alongside the path, not as a single cause.
- **Purpose:** Make clear that belief is one link in a learning process, not a fixed label or a guaranteed outcome.
- **Reading boundary:** Do not label students as fixed/growth types or imply a deterministic causal path.

## Figure 7-2 — 从学习经验到结论之间的证据层次

- **Insertion:** 7.1.3, after the paragraph separating neuroplasticity from claims about teaching effectiveness.
- **Form:** An evidence ladder with five distinct rungs: specified practice; measured neural change; performance on the trained task; transfer to another task; effectiveness across educational settings. Use gaps or conditional connectors between rungs.
- **Purpose:** Show why evidence at one rung does not automatically establish the next.
- **Reading boundary:** No brain image or upward-growth metaphor; the figure should not imply that all measured changes are beneficial.

## Figure 7-3 — 从挫折到下一步学习行动的反馈环路

- **Insertion:** 7.2.5, immediately after the worked example about receiving review feedback.
- **Form:** A loop: event → notice reaction → distinguish facts from interpretation → choose one manageable action → inspect new evidence. Draw emotion and available resources as influences that can enter at multiple points.
- **Purpose:** Connect an emotional response with a practical, revisable learning action without requiring the emotion to disappear first.
- **Reading boundary:** This is an educational reflection aid, not a clinical model or treatment sequence.

## Figure 7-4 — 从同伴经验提取可迁移方法

- **Insertion:** 7.2.6, after the paragraph explaining that comparison should focus on methods rather than personal ranking.
- **Form:** A transfer matrix with rows for task, method, required conditions, and evidence of success; add a three-way decision column: reuse, adapt, or defer.
- **Purpose:** Help readers inspect which parts of another person's process might fit their own task.
- **Reading boundary:** Do not compare students' worth or assume that the visible successful attempt represents every attempt.

## Figure 7-5 — 乔布斯公开叙述中的离开、再起与回归

- **Insertion:** 7.3.1, after the paragraph distinguishing the 2005 prepared speech from an independent company history.
- **Form:** A two-track timeline. Track A lists events he recounts: Apple departure, NeXT and Pixar, Apple's acquisition of NeXT, return to Apple. Track B marks his later interpretation of those events. Visually distinguish event claims from retrospective meaning.
- **Purpose:** Let students see the narrative sequence while preserving the difference between a speaker's account and independent historical evidence.
- **Reading boundary:** Use only details stated in the Stanford transcript; do not add imagined feelings or portray the outcome as a forecast for other people.

## Figure 7-6 — 群体差异与个人变化是两类证据

- **Insertion:** 7.4.3, after the paragraph contrasting population-level and within-person research questions.
- **Form:** Two panels: left, a conceptual comparison of different people at one measurement occasion; right, a conceptual time series for one person across repeated occasions. Label both axes and questions, but use no fabricated measurements or percentages.
- **Purpose:** Make the unit of analysis visible and prevent a population variance estimate from being read as an individual's personal share.
- **Reading boundary:** This is a conceptual diagram, not a reanalysis of data or an estimate of intervention effects.
