## Simulation and Interpretation Boundary

The “地方记忆数字展” and “校园活动导览” are fictional teaching settings. Names, stories, tasks, teams, estimates, outputs, stakeholders, project conditions, and all counts are author-created; no real interviews, participants, locations, model runs, field measurements, deployments, permissions, or institutional outcomes are reported. Figure plans and student tools are original instructional structures. The AI RMF and ACM Code support the principles and distinctions identified above; they do not supply or endorse the chapter’s diagrams, tables, or exercise answers.
